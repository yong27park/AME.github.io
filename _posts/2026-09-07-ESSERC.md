Our collaborative research on wearable devices specifically on a large common-mode interference-tolerant and motion-tolerant analog front-end (AFE) was presented at ESSERC 2026 in Palma de Mallorca, Spain. This work was conducted in close collaboration with Prof. Seong-Jin Kim's group at Sogang University. We look forward to furthering our research in the field of wearable devices!

<br>

Paper title: “A 2.8-NEF 130-mV/140-V DM/CM Artifact-Tolerant Biopotential Amplifier for Two-Electrode Recording System Using Signal Folding”
